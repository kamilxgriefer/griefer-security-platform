GRIEFER

Graph-based Resilient Intelligence Engine for Enforcement & Response

See the attack. Contain the blast. Prove the defense.

Early development

Private development environment. Synthetic-data and simulation-only project.

The platform is not publicly reachable. There is no public API, no public database and no hosted console at this address. A public demonstration will be published later.

GRIEFER is a research and engineering project exploring verifiable, policy-governed cyber defense: correlating weak security signals into a single attack narrative, estimating what an incident could reach, and putting every proposed response through a policy engine that records its reasoning.

It is an early prototype rather than a product. Response actions are simulated — the platform contacts no identity provider, no endpoint and no cloud service — and every scenario it ships with is invented.
